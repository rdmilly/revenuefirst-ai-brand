# RevenueFirst.AI — Brand Guidelines

## Personality
Direct, operator-to-operator, cash-centric, results-first.

## Voice
- Direct: no fluff, every word earns its place
- Specific: dollar amounts not vague claims
- Urgent: leads expire, act now

## Conversion Rules
1. Pain before solution — 78% stat before pitch
2. Red = urgency only — never decorative
3. Green = money up — universal financial signal
4. Specificity converts — '12 jobs in Month 1' not 'more bookings'
5. CTA: 'Get My Free Revenue Audit' not 'Book Consultation'

## Key Numbers
78% | <60s | 3.2x | $47K | 200+

## Logo
Wordmark: Revenue (white) + First (red #dc3545) + .AI (white)
Always on dark navy. Georgia serif bold.
